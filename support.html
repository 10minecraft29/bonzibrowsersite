Quick troubleshooting

Try these before reinstalling.

Step 1

Reload the page

For a single broken site, reload once and verify whether the issue is site-specific or browser-wide.

Step 2

Check per-site privacy controls

Temporarily relax strict third-party or cosmetic settings for that site only.

Step 3

Update filter lists

Open Privacy & BonziShield and update selected lists so the blocker is not relying on stale rules.

Step 4

Use the network logger

Press Alt+Shift+L to see what BonziBlock is allowing or blocking when diagnosing a page.
