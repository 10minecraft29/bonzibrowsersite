About BonziBrowser

A Bonztel browser for the Bonztel ecosystem.

BonziBrowser combines Chromium/Electron web compatibility with Bonztel’s own UI, privacy tools, account system, extensions and product integrations.

Chromium/Electron foundation

BonziBrowser uses Electron’s bundled Chromium engine for website rendering and web-platform compatibility. BonziBrowser adds its own browser chrome, profiles, privacy logic, Sync and extensions around that engine.

BonziBlock + BonziShield

Bonztel’s integrated blocking/privacy layer adds filter-list-based ad protection, tracker controls, site permissions, URL cleanup and diagnostic tools.

Open-source components

The application uses Electron, electron-builder/electron-updater and third-party filter lists such as EasyList/EasyPrivacy when enabled. Distribution should retain their respective licenses/notices.

Bonztel ecosystem

BonziBrowser can expose sidebar shortcuts for BonztelMail, BonztelMessage and Bonzipedia, plus Bonztel Account Sync and local/private assistant tools.
